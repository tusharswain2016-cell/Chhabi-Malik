"Service to the people of Niali is not just my duty — it is my dharma and my greatest honour."

Chhabi Malik is an Indian politician who was elected to the Odisha Legislative Assembly from the Niali constituency in the 2024 state elections as a member of the Bharatiya Janata Party. He assumed office on 4 June 2024, succeeding Pramod Kumar Mallick as the representative of Niali.

Rising through grassroots political engagement in the Niali region, Chhabi Malik has focused his legislative work on rural infrastructure, access to health services, agricultural support, and educational opportunity for the constituents of Niali block in Cuttack district.

Known for his ground-level accessibility, he holds regular Jan Sunwais and personally monitors the progress of every scheme in the constituency. His approach to governance is rooted in listening first, then acting with purpose.

Our Vision

Building a Stronger Niali

Six pillars guiding every decision, every initiative, every vote cast in the legislature on behalf of the people of Niali.

01🌾
Kisan Samridhi

Doubling farmer incomes through MSP support, crop insurance, and direct market linkages — eliminating the middlemen who squeeze our farmers.

02📚
Shiksha for All

Universal quality education with digital classrooms and full scholarship coverage for all BPL families in Niali.

03🏥
Swasth Niali

A primary health centre within 5 km of every village — free diagnostics, maternal care, and mobile medical units.

04💡
Digital Gram

High-speed internet and digital literacy to every panchayat, empowering citizens to access government services from home.

05♻️
Green Niali

Solar-powered streetlights, clean cooking fuel, and river conservation — a constituency that thrives without harming tomorrow.

06👩‍💼
Nari Shakti

30% reservation in local governance, skill centres for women, SHG credit, and zero-tolerance on crimes against women.

Political Career

The Journey So Far

From grassroots activism in Niali to the Odisha Legislative Assembly — a path built on purpose and trust.

24
Roots
Community Leadership in Niali

Began grassroots social and political work in Niali, building connections with farmers, fishing communities, and daily-wage workers. Advocated actively for rural infrastructure and welfare schemes at the block level.

BJP
Joined Bharatiya Janata Party

Aligned with BJP's vision of vikas-led governance and joined the party's Niali unit, contributing to local party building and constituency outreach activities across Cuttack district.

2024
Elected to Odisha Legislative Assembly

Won the Niali assembly seat in the 2024 Odisha Legislative Assembly elections under the BJP banner, succeeding Pramod Kumar Mallick. His election was reported by News18, Kalinga TV, and verified by the Election Commission of India affidavit.

Jun 4
Assumed Office — 4 June 2024

Formally assumed office as Member of the Odisha Legislative Assembly from Niali on 4 June 2024, joining the BJP legislative group in Bhubaneswar. Took up committee responsibilities and began engagement with constituency development programmes.

Now
Serving Niali — Present Day

Focused on constituency development, rural electrification, agro-processing clusters, telemedicine expansion, and ensuring that every central and state government scheme reaches the last household in Niali block.
